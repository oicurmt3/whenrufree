# User Manual

A guide for organisers and participants using When RU Free.

---

## Signing In

Everyone needs a free account to open, join or create calendars and Hubs. There are no PINs.

1. Open any When RU Free page. If you are not signed in, a sign-in panel appears.
2. Choose **Google**, **Facebook** or **Passkey** (fingerprint, face or screen lock). New here? Tap **Create an account with a passkey**.
3. The first time, choose a **username** and a **profile name**:
   - The **username** (3-20 lowercase letters, numbers or `_`) is private and unique. Nobody else sees it; it is used to confirm big changes such as deleting your account.
   - The **profile name** is the name other people see on calendars by default (up to 32 characters). You can use a different name on any single calendar.
4. You stay signed in on that device for 30 days, renewed each time you use the site.

We never receive your email, real name or photo from Google or Facebook - only a private ID that we store scrambled. There are no ads and no tracking.

### Your Profile Page

Tap your name in the top corner of any page to open your profile (`account.html`). There you can:

- Change your username or profile name. A new profile name updates every calendar where you use it (not the ones where you chose a different name). If that name is already taken on a calendar, you are told which one keeps the old name.
- Manage **Ways to sign in**: add Google, Facebook or a passkey for this device, or remove one (you always keep at least one). The Google and Facebook buttons disappear once linked; you can add a passkey on each device you use.
- Usernames are 3-20 lowercase letters, numbers or `_`. A few, such as `admin` and `whenrufree`, are reserved.
- See all your **calendars** and **Hubs** - handy if you lose a link.
- **Sign out** of this device.
- **Delete your account**: shows what will be removed (the calendars and Hubs you organise, and your names on other calendars), then asks you to type your username.

### Names and Hubs from Before Accounts

If you used When RU Free with a PIN before accounts, you can claim your old name or Hub once:

- **Calendar name:** sign in, open the calendar, tap the **People** (group) button and tap your old name ("Yours from before? Tap to claim it"). Enter its old PIN. The name, its role and its availability now belong to your account and the PIN is deleted.
- **Hub:** open the Hub and tap **Claim this Hub**, then enter the old organiser PIN.
- Five wrong PINs in a row lock that name or Hub for 5 minutes, then 15 minutes, 2 hours, 2 days. The site admin can unlock it. Forgot the PIN? Join with a new name and ask the organiser to remove the old one.

## For Organisers

### Creating a Calendar

1. Go to [whenrufree.net](https://whenrufree.net) (or sandgroper.net) and sign in
2. Fill in the form:
   - **Title** (optional): Give your calendar a name, e.g. "Dinner with the team"
   - **Category** (optional): Pick up to 3 icons that represent your event type
   - **Slots**: The time options participants will choose between (e.g. "Morning", "Lunch", "Evening"). You get 1-6 slots. Use the quick-add chips or type your own.
   - **People per slot**: Leave on **No limit** for a normal availability poll. Pick **1**, **2** or **More** (up to 20) to make a booking calendar - see Booking Calendars below.
   - **Statuses**: The response options (default: Yes / Maybe / No). You can customise labels, colours, and scoring weights. Up to 5 statuses.
   - **Date range**: The start and end dates. Maximum 360 days.
   - **Exclude weekends**: Tick this to hide Saturdays and Sundays.
   - **Date filter mode**: Choose whether participants see all dates, only dates you select, or all dates except ones you exclude.
   - **Access emoji** (optional): Turn the switch on and pick one of 35 emoji. Anyone opening the link must pick the same emoji once. Creating inside a Hub that already has an emoji? The calendar uses the Hub's emoji unless you pick its own.
   - **Privacy mode**: Open (everyone sees all choices), Hidden (only you see individual choices), or Blind (nobody sees individual choices).
3. **Organiser name** (optional): leave blank to use your profile name, or type a name just for this calendar. Your account is the organiser - no PIN needed.
4. Tap **Create Calendar**.
5. You will be taken to your new calendar. Organiser tools appear whenever you open it signed in to this account, on any device.

### Sharing Your Calendar

- Tap the **Share** icon in the header to copy or share the calendar link.
- The link looks like: `whenrufree.net/calendar.html?c=3PNpKJTs`
- The link is the invite: anyone signed in who has it can view the calendar and join.
- If you set an access emoji, it is added to the copied, shared, emailed and printed link so people know which one to pick.

### Access Emoji

- As the organiser, tap the **Settings** (gear) icon in the calendar header to turn the emoji on, change it or turn it off. Settings always shows the current emoji.
- Each account picks the emoji once; after that it is remembered on all their devices. People who already joined are never asked.
- Changing or removing the emoji means everyone who has not joined yet has to pick the new one.
- Too many wrong picks lock that internet connection out of the calendar for 5 minutes, then longer.

### Managing Your Calendar

As the organiser, you can:

- **Add a message**: Tap the message area below the rankings to leave a note for participants (max 250 characters).
- **Change sort order**: Toggle between "By Match" (highest score first) and "By Date" (chronological) in the analysis panel. Your choice is saved as the default for all users.
- **Filter dates**: If you chose "only" or "except" mode, tap date column headers to select/deselect which dates participants can see. Changes auto-save.
- **Delete users**: Open the People list and tap the delete icon next to a participant's name.
- **Reset availability**: Use the delete icon to clear your own selections, or the warning icon to reset ALL users (requires typing "RESET" to confirm).
- **Rename**: Tap the **Your name** (person edit) icon to change your name on this calendar, or tap **Use my profile name** to follow your profile name again.

### Choosing a Final Slot

Once enough people have responded, you can lock in a decision:

1. Expand a rank card by tapping "View"
2. Tap the green **Choose** button
3. Confirm the choice in the dialog

This will:
- Display a green "Decided" banner showing the chosen date and slot
- Highlight the chosen slot in the calendar grid with a green outline
- Dim all other slots
- Prevent anyone from changing their availability
- Hide the save button

To undo this, tap the **Reopen** button in the decided banner.

### Booking Calendars

Use a booking calendar when each slot can only be taken by a set number of people - interviews, appointments, rosters, parent-teacher nights.

- **Setting up:** under Slots, set **People per slot** to 1, 2 or More (3-20). **Bookings per person** then appears below it: how many slots each person can book (No limit / 1 / 2 / More; default 1). With a limit, choose how it is counted: **Per day** (default) or **In total**. With 1 per day, someone can book one time on Monday and another on Tuesday, but not two times on Monday - good for court or room hire. With 1 in total, each person gets one slot in the whole calendar - good for interviews or workshop sign-ups; people see "Mine (1 of 1)" on the Bookings card. Below that, set the **Booking label** (default "Booked") and its colour, plus the colour and name for **Partly booked** (default orange; only when a slot holds 2 or more people) and **Full** (default red) slots. Rename them like options, e.g. "Filling up" or "Taken"; the names show in the legend, and the Full name also shows on full slots. The Options section is hidden, because bookings use this one label. Blind privacy is not available - Open shows everyone who booked each slot; Hidden shows names only to you, and others just see which slots are taken.
- **Booking settings cannot be changed** after the calendar is created.
- **How people book:** they join the calendar, then tap a free slot to mark it Booked; tapping it again un-books it. Like a normal calendar, changes auto-save after a few seconds, or tap **Submit** to save now. The **Book** / **Cancel** buttons on the Bookings card save straight away. A full slot shows "This slot is taken", and the per-person limit counts unsaved taps too. When a slot holds 2 or more people, each cell starts with how many have booked so far, e.g. "1/2" or "3/8". If two people tap the last place at the same moment, only the first gets it; the other sees "Just taken by someone else".
- **Bookings card** (instead of Best Match): shows "N of M booked" and a two-way toggle. Participants get **Mine / Free**: Mine lists your own bookings in date order (with a count, e.g. "Mine (2)"), Free lists the slots still open. The organiser gets **Booked / Free** instead, which lists every booked slot. The card always opens on Mine (or Booked for the organiser), showing 2 rows; tap 10 or All to see more. Each row looks like a Best Match card: tap **View** to see who booked it (as privacy allows) and the buttons - **Book** on free slots, **Add** / **Download** / **Share** / **Cancel** on yours (other people's bookings have no buttons).
- **As the organiser** you can book as many slots as you like (to block times off), remove anyone's booking with the x next to their name (tap View on the row), and tap **Close bookings** to stop all changes. A "Bookings closed" banner then shows for everyone; tap **Reopen** in it to allow changes again.
- The calendar refreshes itself every 30 seconds while it is open, and whenever you come back to the tab.

### Template Links

You can create pre-configured calendar links from the Ideas page. These pre-fill the creation form with your chosen settings. The URL format is:

```
index.html?template=1&title=...&category=...&slots=...&statuses=...&range=1week
```

Add `&cap=1` (people per slot, 1-20) to make a booking calendar, and optionally `&per=2` (bookings per person, counted per day; `0` = no limit, default 1) and `&total=1` (count `per` across the whole calendar instead). With `cap`, the first `statuses` label becomes the booking label. The Templates page has ready-made booking examples (interviews, parent-teacher nights, workshops, tours, volunteer rosters, court hire, hot desks, gym, school-run seats, pet care, holiday house, shared car, meeting room) in its Bookings section.

### Calendar Hubs

A **Hub** gives your group one link to find all related calendars (e.g. club fixtures, team dinners, committee meetings).

**Creating a Hub**

1. Open **Create a Hub** from the home page footer or `hub.html?create=1` and sign in
2. Enter the Hub name and, optionally, the organiser name shown on the Hub (blank = your profile name)
3. Optional: turn on **Access emoji** and pick one. It guards the Hub and every calendar in it that has no emoji of its own
4. Save your Hub link from the save panel (copy, download, Share, Email, or Poster). It is also listed on your profile page
5. Tap **Continue to Hub**

**Managing a Hub**

- The account that created the Hub is its organiser. Open the Hub signed in to that account to get Share, Email, Poster, Download, **Settings** (the Hub's access emoji) and removing calendars from the Hub
- An old Hub (created with a PIN before accounts) shows **Claim this Hub**; see "Names and Hubs from Before Accounts"

**Adding calendars to a Hub**

- **New calendar:** Anyone with the Hub link can tap **Create new calendar in this Hub**
- **Existing calendar:** As that calendar's organiser, open the Hub bar on the calendar page and enter the Hub code or link

**Removing a calendar from a Hub**

- The Hub organiser (on the Hub page), or the calendar's own organiser (from the calendar page Hub bar)

### Go to code or link

Use **Go to code** (`go/`) to open a calendar or Hub when you have a code or full link:

1. Choose **Calendar** or **Hub**
2. Paste the code (e.g. `3PNpKJTs`) or full URL
3. Tap **Go**

## For Participants

### Joining via a Hub

1. Open the Hub link shared by the organiser (`hub.html?g=...`) and sign in
2. Pick the calendar you need from the list
3. Join the calendar as usual

### Picking the Access Emoji

If the organiser set an emoji, a grid of 35 emoji appears before the calendar or Hub opens. Pick the one the organiser gave you (it is usually written next to the link) and tap **Open**. Your account remembers it, so you are not asked again on other devices. Three wrong picks lock you out for a few minutes, then longer. If you do not know it, ask the organiser.

### Joining a Calendar

1. Open the calendar link shared by the organiser and sign in (pick the access emoji if asked)
2. The **Join this calendar** window opens with your profile name filled in. Keep it, or type a different name just for this calendar
3. Tap **Join**

Only your account can change your availability. If the name is already used on that calendar, add a letter or number.

### Marking Your Availability

1. After joining, tap any time slot in the calendar grid to cycle through statuses (e.g. Yes -> Maybe -> No -> clear)
2. The colour legend at the top shows what each colour means
3. Changes auto-save after 4.5 seconds of inactivity, or tap the save icon manually
4. A coloured left border on slots shows the organiser's choice (except in Blind mode)

### Returning Later

- Open the same calendar link, or find it on your profile page
- While you are signed in (30 days per device, renewed on use), you go straight to your name
- On a new device, sign in with the same Google, Facebook or passkey and you are back

### Passkeys (Fingerprint / Face Unlock)

A passkey signs you in with your fingerprint, face, Windows Hello or device screen lock - no password.

- **New account:** on the sign-in panel tap **New here? Create an account with a passkey**
- **Existing account:** open your profile page, go to **Ways to sign in** and tap **Add a passkey**
- **Sign in:** tap **Sign in with a passkey** on the sign-in panel and pick your account
- Your fingerprint or face never leaves your device; the site only stores a public key
- Lost a device? Remove its passkey on your profile page, as long as you keep another sign-in method

### Viewing Results

- The **Best Match** panel at the top shows ranked date+slot combinations
- Tap "View" on a rank card to see who responded and their statuses
- You can add the date to Google Calendar or download an ICS file from expanded cards
- If the organiser has locked in a choice, you will see a green "Decided" banner

### Privacy Modes

Your calendar may be in one of three privacy modes:

- **Open**: You can see everyone's choices (after joining)
- **Hidden**: Only the organiser can see individual choices. You see aggregated counts only.
- **Blind**: Nobody can see individual choices, not even the organiser. True anonymity.

The privacy mode is shown by an icon next to the calendar title (eye, hidden eye, or lock).

---

## Tips

- Sign out on shared computers (profile page > **Sign out**).
- The calendar cleans up automatically after 40 days of inactivity (no saves by anyone). The organiser can ask the site admin to make it permanent.
- Accounts with no calendars or Hubs are deleted after 2 years without use.
- Slot names support letters, numbers, spaces, hyphens, and periods. Max 20 characters.
- Names support letters, numbers, spaces, apostrophes, and hyphens. Max 32 characters.
