Calendars with no Submit/Save activity (i.e. no last_activity update) for this many days will be automatically deleted by the daily cleanup job. Permanent calendars are always protected regardless of this setting — toggle the Permanent switch on the Calendars tab to keep a specific calendar forever.

1 - 3650 (default 30)

Hubs with no activity for this many days are automatically deleted by the same cleanup job.
