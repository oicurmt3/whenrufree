Everything you need to know about using When RU Free? to coordinate group availability.

rocket_launch Getting Started

How it works

When RU Free? is a simple group availability tool. An organiser creates a calendar, shares the link with participants, and everyone marks their availability. The app automatically ranks the best dates based on responses.

  1. Create a calendar - Set a title, date range, time slots, and status options
  2. Share the link - Send the unique URL to your group
  3. Add availability - Each person signs in, joins the calendar, and marks their slots
  4. See results - The "Best Catch-Up Dates" section ranks optimal times

Opening a link by code

If someone gave you a calendar or Hub code (or a full URL), use the Go page. Choose Calendar or Hub, paste the code or link, and tap Go.

hub Calendar Hubs

A Hub gives your group one link to find all related calendars — useful for clubs, teams, or committees running multiple polls.

  1. Create a Hub — give it a name (Create a Hub); your account is the organiser
  2. Save the Hub link when prompted — it is also listed on your profile page
  3. Add calendars — anyone with the Hub link can create a new calendar in the Hub; calendar organisers can attach existing calendars via Add to Hub on the calendar page
  4. Share once — participants pick which calendar to open from the Hub list
Do I need to be the Hub organiser to create a calendar in a Hub? expand_more

No. Anyone signed in with the Hub link can create a new calendar in that Hub. Only the organiser's account can manage the Hub (remove calendars, Settings, Share/Email/Poster/Download).

How do I add an existing calendar to a Hub? expand_more

Open the calendar signed in with the account that created it. On the calendar page, use the Hub bar and tap Add to Hub. Enter the Hub code or full Hub link.

I lost my Hub link — can you recover it? expand_more

Sign in and open your profile page: it lists the Hubs you organise or have opened, and the calendars you have joined. We cannot look up links for anyone else.

person For Participants

Adding your availability

  1. Sign in (Google, Facebook or passkey) and open the calendar link
  2. Tap Join. Your profile name is filled in; change it for this calendar if you like
  3. Click on time slots to cycle through status options (e.g., Yes -> Maybe -> No -> blank)
  4. Your changes auto-save after a few seconds, or click check to save immediately

Understanding the slots

Each slot may show a coloured left border. This indicates the organiser's choice for that slot, helping you see which times they prefer. They appear on Open calendars, unless the organiser has turned them off.

Changing your responses

Simply click any slot to cycle through the options again. To clear a slot entirely, keep clicking until it returns to the default grey (no selection).

Resetting your availability

Click the delete trash icon to reset ALL your availability. This requires double confirmation (you must type "RESET") to prevent accidents.

person_shield For Organisers (Admin)

As the calendar creator, you have additional features:

share Share

Share the calendar link via your device's share menu or copy to clipboard.

person_edit Your name here

Change the name you use on this calendar: open People and tap the edit icon on your line (anyone who has joined can do this; saved availability is kept).

delete Reset Mine

Clear all YOUR availability data. Requires typing "RESET" to confirm.

warning Reset All

Clear ALL availability for ALL users. Requires typing "RESET" to confirm.

hub Add to Hub

Attach this calendar to a Hub using the Hub code or link. Remove from Hub from the same bar.

check_circle Choose / Finalise

Lock in a chosen date and slot from the rankings panel. Freezes the calendar. Tap "Reopen" to undo.

Organiser status indicator

When you (the organiser) set your availability, other participants will see a coloured left border on their slots indicating your preferences. This helps guide participants to times that work for you. This works on Open calendars. If you'd rather not steer people's choices, turn it off under settings Calendar settings.

trophy Best Dates Ranking

The ranking automatically calculates the best meeting times based on everyone's responses. Each status option has a "weight":

  • Yes (or first option) = highest weight
  • Maybe (or second option) = medium weight
  • No (or third+ options) = lowest weight

Slots with the highest combined weights appear at the top. Click "View" on any ranking to see who responded and their individual statuses.

Adding to your calendar

Once you've decided on a date, expand the ranking card and use:

  • Add to Google - Opens Google Calendar with the event pre-filled
  • .ics Download - Downloads a calendar file for Outlook, Apple Calendar, etc.
  • Choose (organiser only) - Locks in this date+slot as the final decision and freezes the calendar

contact_support Frequently Asked Questions

Why do I need to sign in? expand_more

So only you can change your availability, without remembering a PIN for every calendar. Your profile page also lists all your calendars and Hubs, so a lost link is no longer lost.

Sign in with Google, Facebook or a passkey (fingerprint, face or screen lock). We never receive your email, name or photo from Google or Facebook — only a private ID that we keep scrambled. There are no ads and no tracking.

You can look around the home page without signing in. Tap Sign in at the top, or anything in the create form, when you're ready.

What is my username for? expand_more

It is private: only you (and the site admin) see it. It is used to confirm big actions such as deleting your account. Other people only see your profile name, or the name you chose for a particular calendar.

I had a name with a PIN before accounts. What now? expand_more

Sign in, open the calendar, tap the group People button and tap your old name (it says Tap to claim it). Enter its old PIN once. The name and its availability then belong to your account and the PIN is deleted.

Old Hubs work the same way: open the Hub and tap Claim this Hub, then enter the old organiser PIN.

Five wrong PINs in a row lock that name for 5 minutes (then longer). The site admin can unlock it. If you forgot the PIN, join again with a new name and ask the organiser to remove the old one.

How do I use a passkey (fingerprint or face)? expand_more

On the sign-in screen tap Sign in with a passkey to use one you already have, or New here? Create an account with a passkey. If you signed in another way, tap Add a passkey under Ways to sign in on your profile page.

Your fingerprint or face never leaves your device. You can remove a lost device's passkey on the profile page, as long as one sign-in method remains.

How do I delete my account? expand_more

Open your profile page, scroll to Delete account, type your username and confirm. This removes your account, your names on calendars, and every calendar and Hub you organise. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Can I use it as a booking form (one person per slot)? expand_more

Yes. When creating the calendar, set People per slot (under Slots) to 1, 2 or More (up to 20), and choose Bookings per person - how many slots each person can book, counted Per day (e.g. court hire) or In total across the whole calendar (e.g. one interview per candidate). People tap a free slot to book it (tap again to un-book) and it saves like any calendar, first come, first served. Slots booked by other people show in red, and if two people tap the last place at the same moment only the first gets it.

The organiser can remove anyone's booking and use Close bookings to stop changes. Booking settings cannot be changed after the calendar is created. Blind privacy is not available for booking calendars, because the organiser needs to see who booked.

Each account can have one name per calendar, so the per-person limit applies per account. Someone with a second account could still book more; the organiser sees every name and can remove extra bookings.

What is the access emoji? expand_more

An optional extra lock on top of the secret link. The organiser picks one of 35 emoji, and anyone opening the calendar or Hub must pick the same one first. The emoji is written next to the link when the organiser copies, shares, emails or prints it. Your account remembers it for 30 days, and people who have joined the calendar (or organise the Hub) never need it.

Organisers turn it on when creating the calendar (above Privacy Mode) or Hub, or later with the Settings (gear) button. A calendar in a Hub uses the Hub's emoji unless it has its own. Three wrong picks lock that device out for 5 minutes, then longer.

What is Invite only? expand_more

When creating a calendar or Hub, Who can join sets how people get in. Direct link (the default) lets anyone signed in with the link open it. With Invite only, people with the link tap Request access and the organiser approves or declines each one. Approved people then join with their own name as usual. This is chosen when creating and can't be changed later.

Organisers see requests in the People list (the number on the People button shows how many are waiting), or on the Hub page for an Invite only Hub. Approving someone for a Hub opens every calendar in it, and calendars created inside an Invite only Hub are always Invite only. If there is also an access emoji, people pick it before they can request access; once approved they are never asked again.

While you wait, the calendar or Hub is listed on your profile under Waiting for approval. Requests that aren't answered within 30 days expire, and you can ask again. If the organiser declines, you can't ask again, but they can still approve you later.

Can the organiser block someone? expand_more

Yes, on any calendar. In the People list the organiser taps the more menu beside a name to open Manage person, where they choose Block and confirm. That person sees "The organiser has blocked you from this calendar" and can't open the calendar, and their answers are left out of the calendar, Best Match and totals. Bookings they made still hold their places. Choose Unblock the same way to let them back in - nothing is deleted. The same menu can delete a person or report them to the super admin. On an Invite only Hub, the organiser can also block an approved person from the whole Hub on the Hub page.

Can I see one person's whole calendar? expand_more

Yes, where the privacy mode allows it. Tap the group People button, then the visibility eye beside a name. Their Slots fill the calendar, read only, with a bar above it showing whose they are. Tap the eye again (now visibility_off), the bar's close, or press Escape to go back to yours; your own unsaved changes are kept.

On Open calendars anyone who has joined can do this; on Hidden calendars only the organiser; on Blind calendars nobody. It isn't available on booking calendars or for people the organiser has blocked.

I don't know or forgot the emoji. What now? expand_more

Ask the organiser - it is usually in the message or poster with the link. Organisers can see and change it in Settings on any device where they are signed in. If the organiser forgot it too, the site admin can remove it.

How long do calendars last? expand_more

Calendars with no activity (saves) are automatically deleted after a period of time to reduce data storage. Make sure someone saves their availability periodically to keep the calendar active, or export your results before the calendar expires.

Can I edit the calendar settings after creation? expand_more

Currently, calendar settings (title, dates, slots, status options) cannot be changed after creation. Create a new calendar if you need different settings.

Is my data secure? expand_more

The calendar link is the invite - anyone signed in with the link can view and join, unless the organiser made it Invite only (then they approve each person). Only share links with trusted participants. Only your account can change your entry. Do not enter sensitive or private information.

What do the privacy modes mean? expand_more

When creating a calendar, you can choose from three privacy modes:

  • visibility Open - All users can see each other's choices. This is the default and works best when everyone should see who's available when.
  • visibility_off Hidden - Users cannot see each other's choices, but the organiser can still see all individual responses. Useful for voting or when you don't want participants to influence each other. Tick Hide names too to also keep the People list private.
  • lock Blind - All choices are completely private. Even the organiser cannot see individual responses - only aggregate counts (e.g., "3 Yes, 2 Maybe"). Names are private too. Perfect for anonymous voting or sensitive surveys.

In all modes, aggregate counts are always visible so everyone can see the overall results.

When names are private (Blind, or Hidden with Hide names too), the People list only shows each person their own name, the organiser and how many people have joined. The organiser always sees everyone.

What does "Date display" do? expand_more

When creating a calendar, the organiser can choose how dates are shown to participants:

  • date_range All dates - Every date in the range is shown. This is the default.
  • event_available Only chosen - Participants see only the dates the organiser selects. After creating the calendar, the organiser clicks date headers to pick which dates are visible.
  • event_busy All except - Participants see all dates except the ones the organiser selects. Useful for blocking out dates that are already taken.

The organiser always sees all dates (with selected ones highlighted in blue) so they can manage their selections. Date filter selections auto-save to the server.

How does the organiser select dates for filtering? expand_more

After creating a calendar with "Only chosen" or "All except" mode, the organiser logs in and clicks the date headers (the day number/name at the top of each column) to toggle dates on and off. Selected dates appear with a blue highlight that is only visible to the organiser. A banner at the top shows the current filter mode and how many dates are selected.

What does the green "Decided" banner mean? expand_more

The organiser has locked in a final date and time slot. The calendar is now frozen -- no one can change their availability. The chosen slot is highlighted in the calendar grid with a green outline, and all other slots are dimmed. If the organiser changes their mind, they can tap "Reopen" to allow editing again.

How does the organiser finalise a date? expand_more

The organiser expands a rank card by tapping "View", then taps the green Choose button. After confirming, the calendar is frozen and a green "Decided" banner appears. To undo, tap the "Reopen" button in the banner.

Why don't I see the organiser dots? expand_more

Organiser indicators (coloured left borders on slots) only appear when the organiser has set their availability for a slot. If you ARE the organiser, you won't see your own indicators (you already know your preferences!). They are only visible to other participants, and only on Open calendars. The organiser can also turn them off in Calendar settings.

Does my availability save automatically? expand_more

Yes! Changes auto-save after about 4-5 seconds of inactivity. You'll see the save button spin briefly when saving. You can also click the check button to save immediately.

What do the different status colours mean? expand_more

The legend at the top of the calendar shows what each colour means. By default:

  • Green = Yes / Available
  • Yellow/Orange = Maybe / Tentative
  • Red/Blue = No / Unavailable
  • Grey = No response yet

Organisers can customise these options when creating the calendar.

tooltip_2 Tips

  • Find your calendars on your profile - Every calendar you join is listed on your profile page
  • Use the Go page - Open Go to code if you have a calendar or Hub code or URL
  • Add a second sign-in method - For example a passkey as well as Google, so you can still get in if one stops working
  • Check the organiser indicator - The coloured left border on slots shows which times the organiser prefers
  • View "Top 10" - Click the "Top 10" button to see more ranked date options
  • Use date filtering - Organisers can narrow the visible dates using "Only chosen" or "All except" modes
  • Export early - Calendars may be deleted after inactivity, so save important outcomes
