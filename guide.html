overview Overview

When RU Free? is a simple group availability tool. An organiser creates a calendar, shares the link with participants, and everyone marks their availability. The app automatically ranks the best dates based on responses. Your calendar has its own unique link with your time slots, availabiity options and date range. Everyone uses the same link and adds their name inside the calendar.

  1. Create a calendar - Set a title, date range, time slots, and status options
  2. Share the link - Send the unique URL to your group
  3. Add availability - Each person signs in, joins the calendar, and marks their slots
  4. See results - The "Best Match" section ranks optimal times

Creating a calendar takes less than 2 minutes. Once created, you'll get a unique link to share with your group. Bookmark this link -- it is also listed on your profile page while you are signed in.

account_circle Signing in

Everyone needs a free account to open, join or create calendars and Hubs. There are no PINs to remember. You can look around the home page first: tap Sign in at the top, or anything in the create form, when you are ready.

  1. Sign in with Google, Facebook or a passkey (fingerprint, face or screen lock). We never receive your email, name or photo — only a private ID we keep scrambled.
  2. First time: choose a private username (only you see it) and a profile name (what others see on calendars you join).
  3. Profile page (tap your name badge at the top of any page): change your profile name, see all your calendars and Hubs, add or remove sign-in methods, sign out, or delete your account.
  4. Name per calendar: your profile name is used when you join, but you can use a different name on any calendar. Changing your profile name updates every calendar where you kept the profile name.
  5. You stay signed in for 30 days on each device, renewed while you use the site.

Names and Hubs from before accounts can be claimed once with their old PIN: on the calendar, tap People and your old name; on a Hub, tap Claim this Hub. The PIN is then deleted.

hub Calendar Hubs

A Hub gives your group one link to find all related calendars — useful when you run multiple polls or events (e.g. training, social, AGM).

  1. Create a Hub from the home page or Create a Hub — set a name and optionally an Access emoji that visitors must pick before the Hub (and its calendars) open. Your account is the organiser.
  2. Save your Hub link when prompted (copy, download .txt, email, share, or poster). It is also listed on your profile page.
  3. Add calendars — anyone signed in with the Hub link can tap Create new calendar in this Hub. To attach an existing calendar, open it with the account that created it and use Add to Hub on the calendar page.
  4. Share the Hub link with participants — they pick which calendar to open and join it there
  5. Copy link or code — on the Hub page, anyone can copy the Hub link or code. Share, Email, Poster, Download, Settings and removing calendars are only shown to the organiser's account.

Hub URL format: hub.html?g=Ab12Cd34. Use the Go page to open a calendar or Hub by pasting a code or full link.

Step 2

schedule Slots

Define the time options that appear for each day. These are the slots participants will mark their availability for.

Quick Add

Click preset buttons to quickly add common slots:

  • Morning Afternoon Evening - General time periods
  • Lunch Dinner - Meal times
  • All Day - Single full-day slot

Custom Slots

Add custom slots with specific times or descriptions (e.g., "9am-10am", "After work", "Session 1"). You can have 1 to 6 slots per day.

Tip: Keep slot names short (under 15 characters) for best display on mobile devices.
Step 3

toggle_on Options

These are the choices people cycle through when they click a slot. The first option is typically "yes/available" and scores highest in rankings.

Preset Status Sets

Click a preset to quickly set up common status combinations:

Preset Options
Yes / No / Maybe Classic three-way choice (default)
Available / Unavailable Simple two-way choice
Yes Assume No unless you choose Yes
No Assume Yes unless you choose No
Free / Busy / Tentative Calendar-style availability
In / Out / Maybe Casual attendance tracking
Attending / Not Attending Formal RSVP style

Scoring Mode

Choose how the "Best Match" is calculated:

  • Rank mode: Position determines score (top option = highest score)
  • Weights mode: Manually assign point values (0-5) to each status

Custom Colours

Click the colour picker next to each option to choose a custom colour. The text colour will automatically adjust for readability.

Default Option

Each option row ends in a checkbox. Tick one to make it the default option: everyone's slots start on that option until they change them, so nobody starts from a blank slot. Only one option can be the default, and ticking it hides the No option row below the options.

No Option Colour

The No option row at the bottom of the options list sets the colour of a slot nobody has answered yet. Pick one of five greys. Leave every checkbox unticked and slots start un-answered in this colour; tick a default option and the row disappears, because no slot can be un-answered. Booking calendars never offer a default option -- their slots are free until somebody books them.

Tip: You can have 1 to 5 status options. The order matters -- put your "best" response first.
Step 4

date_range Date Range

Select the start and end dates for your calendar. Each day in this range will show your time slots.

Quick Presets

Click a preset to automatically set dates from today:

  • 1 day - Single day (e.g., for voting)
  • 1 week 2 weeks - Short-term planning
  • 1 month - Longer-term coordination

Exclude Weekends

Tick the Exclude (Sat/Sun) checkbox to hide weekends from the calendar. Useful for work meetings.

Date Display Mode

Control which dates participants can see:

Mode Description
date_range All dates Show every date in the range (default)
event_available Only chosen Participants see only dates you select (click date headers after creation)
event_busy All except Hide specific dates from participants (e.g., dates already booked)
Note: Maximum range is 1 year. Dates must be between 2026 and 2030.
Step 5

admin_panel_settings Organiser (Admin)

As the organiser, you have special privileges. Set up your identity here.

Your name on this calendar

Optional. Leave it blank to use your profile name. You're labelled Organiser in the People list, and your account badge at the top turns dark with an admin_panel_settings icon on your calendars.

The account that creates the calendar is its organiser — no PIN needed. Organiser-only features (Share, Settings, Reset All, removing people, Choose) appear whenever you open the calendar signed in to that account.

People per Slot (booking)

Leave People per slot on No limit for a normal availability poll. Choose 1, 2 or More (up to 20) to turn the calendar into a booking form: each slot can then be taken by that many people, first come, first served. Bookings per person appears below it: how many slots each person can book (default 1), counted Per day (default) or In total across the whole calendar. Under that you can rename the Booking label (default "Booked") and pick its colour; the Options section is hidden while booking. People tap a free slot to book it (tap again to un-book), and it saves like any calendar; you can remove bookings and Close bookings from the Bookings card. Booking settings cannot be changed later. Blind privacy is not available for booking calendars, because you need to see who booked.

Joining

Anyone signed in with the link can open and join the calendar. Only share links with people you trust.

Access Emoji (optional)

Turn on the Access emoji switch and pick one of 35 emoji. Anyone opening your calendar link must pick the same emoji before they can see it. It is added automatically when you copy, share, email or print the link. You can change it or turn it off later with the Settings (gear) button on the calendar. When creating inside a Hub that has an emoji, the calendar uses the Hub's emoji unless you pick its own.

Privacy Mode

Choose how much participants can see of each other's responses:

Mode What Users See What Organiser Sees
visibility Open All choices from everyone (once joined) All choices from everyone
visibility_off Hidden Only their own choices All choices from everyone
lock Blind Only aggregate counts Only aggregate counts

Important: You must join the calendar to see other participants' individual choices in the rankings section. Before joining you only see aggregate counts (e.g., "3 Yes, 2 Maybe"). Privacy mode cannot be changed after the calendar is created.

Names: on Blind calendars, and on Hidden calendars unless the organiser switches on Show everyone who has joined, the People list shows each person only their own name, the organiser and how many people have joined. The organiser always sees everyone.

hourglass_bottom After Creating Your Calendar

Once you click "Create New Calendar", you'll be redirected to your new calendar. Here's what to do:

  1. Bookmark the page
    The URL contains a unique 8-character token (e.g., calendar.html?c=Ab3dEf9K) and is the invite you share. If you lose it, open your profile page while signed in to the same account: every calendar you organise is listed there.
    • iPhone: Share... "Add Bookmark" or "Add to Home Screen"
    • Android: Menu... "Add to Home screen"
    • Desktop: Press Ctrl+D (Windows) or Cmd+D (Mac)
  2. Add your own availability
    Click on slots to set your availability first - on Open calendars this creates the coloured left borders that guide participants (visible only to people who have joined). Rather not steer people? Turn them off under settings Calendar settings.
  3. Select your date filter (if applicable)
    If you chose "Only chosen" or "All except" mode, click the date headers to select which dates participants will see. Selected dates turn blue. Your selections auto-save.
  4. Share the link
    Use the share Share button to send the calendar to your group. The short URL is easy to share via text or messaging apps.
Tip: Copy and save your calendar link to share it. You can also find it later on your profile page.

person For Participants

Joining via a Hub

If you received a Hub link, open it and pick the calendar you need from the list. Then join that calendar as usual.

Adding your availability

  1. Sign in (Google, Facebook or passkey) and open the calendar link
  2. Tap Join (or any slot). Your profile name is filled in; change it for this calendar if you like
  3. Click on time slots to cycle through status options (e.g., Yes -> Maybe -> No -> blank)
  4. Your changes auto-save after a few seconds, or click check to save immediately

People, your name, and signing out

Tap the group People button to see everyone on the calendar. Your name is listed first: tap it to go back to your own Slots, or tap the person_edit button on your line to change the name you use on this calendar, or go back to your profile name. To sign out, open your profile page from the name badge at the top and tap Sign out.

See someone's calendar: tap the visibility eye beside a name to show that person's Slots in the calendar, read only. Tap it again (visibility_off) to hide them. A bar above the calendar shows whose it is; tap its close (or press Escape) to go back to yours. Your own unsaved changes are kept. This follows the privacy mode: on Open calendars anyone who has joined can do it, on Hidden calendars only the organiser, and on Blind calendars nobody. It isn't available on booking calendars.

Privacy note: You must join the calendar to see other participants' individual choices in the rankings. Until then you only see aggregate counts (e.g., "3 Yes, 2 Maybe").

Reporting someone

If another person on the calendar is behaving badly or is using an inappropriate name, open People, find their name and tap the flag flag. Pick a reason and add a short note if you like.

A report about another participant goes to the calendar's organiser, who sees who reported it. That person is never told who reported them, and nothing happens to them until the organiser decides. A report about the organiser goes straight to the super admin, because an organiser cannot decide a complaint about themselves; the organiser does not see it. You can report the same person on the same calendar once while the report is open, and not again straight after it is decided. Reporting never blocks or removes anyone.

Leaving a calendar

On your profile page, tap Leave calendar beside a calendar to remove your name and every choice you made. The calendar stays for everyone else, and opening the link again joins you back with a blank calendar. Organisers get Delete calendar on their own calendars instead, which removes the calendar for everyone and stops the link working. Both ask for confirmation first.

Understanding the slots

Each slot may show a coloured left border. This indicates the organiser's choice for that slot, helping you see which times they prefer. You must have joined the calendar to see these indicators. They appear on Open calendars, unless the organiser has turned them off in Calendar settings.

Changing your responses

Simply click any slot to cycle through the options again. To clear a slot entirely, keep clicking until it returns to the default grey (no selection).

Resetting your availability

Click the delete trash icon to reset ALL your availability. This requires double confirmation (you must type "RESET") to prevent accidents.

trophy Best Match Rankings

The ranking automatically calculates the best meeting times based on everyone's responses. How scores are calculated depends on the scoring mode set when the calendar was created:

Rank Mode (default)

Position determines score - the first option scores highest:

  • 1st option (e.g., Yes) = highest score
  • 2nd option (e.g., Maybe) = medium score
  • 3rd+ options (e.g., No) = lowest score

Weights Mode

The organiser manually assigns point values (0-5) to each option:

  • Equal weights (e.g., all set to 1) - Every choice counts the same. Best for voting where you want to count how many people selected each option, not rank preferences.
  • Custom weights (e.g., 3, 2, 1, 0) - Fine-tune scoring. For example, "Confirmed = 3" counts more than "Interested = 2".

How Rankings Work

Each date/slot combination gets a total score by adding up everyone's weighted responses. Slots with the highest combined scores appear at the top. Click "View" on any ranking to see who responded and their individual statuses.

Sorting Options

Everyone who has joined can change how rankings are displayed using the toggle at the top of the rankings section:

  • By Match - Best scores first (default). Shows the most popular dates at the top.
  • By Date - Chronological order. Shows earliest dates first, regardless of score.

The organiser's sort preference is saved as the default that all users see when they first open the calendar. Individual users can change the toggle during their session. Use the 2 / 10 / All buttons to control how many results are shown.

Adding to your calendar

Once you've decided on a date, expand the ranking card and use:

  • Add - Opens Google Calendar with the event pre-filled
  • Download - Downloads a .ics calendar file for Outlook, Apple Calendar, etc.
  • Share - Share the date, time slot, and roster details via your device's share menu or copy to clipboard

check_circle Choosing a Final Slot (Organiser)

Once enough people have responded, the organiser can lock in a final decision directly from the rankings panel.

  1. Make sure you are signed in to the account that created the calendar
  2. Expand a rank card by tapping View
  3. Tap the green Choose button
  4. Confirm the choice in the dialog that appears

What happens when a slot is chosen

  • A green "Decided" banner appears at the top showing the chosen date and slot
  • The chosen slot is highlighted with a green outline in the calendar grid
  • All other slots are dimmed and cannot be clicked
  • The save button is hidden -- no one can change their availability
  • The chosen rank card is pinned to the top of the rankings with a green border
  • New participants can still join, but they cannot select slots

Reopening a calendar

If plans change, the organiser can tap the Reopen button in the decided banner. This removes the lock and allows everyone to edit their availability again. A confirmation dialog prevents accidental reopening.

Tip: The freeze is enforced on the server -- even if someone tries to save changes via the browser console, the server will reject them.

lightbulb_2 Tips for Success

  • Keep it simple - 3-4 time slots and 2-3 status options work best
  • Set your availability first - on Open calendars participants will see your preferences as coloured left borders (you can turn these off in Calendar settings)
  • Choose a clear title - helps participants know what they're responding to
  • Share widely - send reminders to people who haven't responded
  • Check the rankings - the "Best Match" section automatically finds optimal times
  • Export results - use the Google Calendar or .ics download to save the final decision
  • Lock it in - once everyone has responded, use the Choose button to finalise and freeze the calendar
  • Stay active - calendars with no saves for 40 days are automatically deleted

warning Things You Can't Change After Creation

Once a calendar is created, these settings are fixed:

  • Calendar title
  • Categories
  • Time slots
  • Status options and colours
  • Default option and the "No option" colour
  • Date range
  • Weekend exclusion setting
  • Date display mode (All dates / Only chosen / All except)
  • Privacy mode

If you need different settings, you'll need to create a new calendar.

contact_support Troubleshooting

Why do I need to sign in? expand_more

So only you can change your availability, without remembering a PIN for every calendar. Your profile page also lists all your calendars and Hubs, so a lost link is no longer lost.

Sign in with Google, Facebook or a passkey (fingerprint, face or screen lock). We never receive your email, name or photo from Google or Facebook — only a private ID that we keep scrambled. There are no ads and no tracking.

What is my username for? expand_more

It is private: only you (and the site admin) see it. It is used to confirm big actions such as deleting your account. Other people only see your profile name, or the name you chose for a particular calendar.

I had a name with a PIN before accounts. What now? expand_more

Sign in, open the calendar, tap the group People button and tap your old name (it says Tap to claim it). Enter its old PIN once. The name and its availability then belong to your account and the PIN is deleted.

Old Hubs work the same way: open the Hub and tap Claim this Hub, then enter the old organiser PIN.

Five wrong PINs in a row lock that name for 5 minutes (then longer). The site admin can unlock it. If you forgot the PIN, join again with a new name and ask the organiser to remove the old one.

How do I use a passkey (fingerprint or face)? expand_more

On the sign-in screen tap Sign in with a passkey to use one you already have, or New here? Create an account with a passkey. If you signed in another way, tap Add a passkey under Ways to sign in on your profile page.

Your fingerprint or face never leaves your device. You can remove a lost device's passkey on the profile page, as long as one sign-in method remains.

How do I delete my account? expand_more

Open your profile page, scroll to Delete account, type your username and confirm. This removes your account, your names on calendars, and every calendar and Hub you organise. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Can I use it as a booking form (one person per slot)? expand_more

Yes. Set People per slot (under Slots) to 1, 2 or More when creating the calendar. People tap a free slot to book it, first come, first served; slots booked by other people show in red. The organiser can remove bookings and use Close bookings to stop changes.

What is the access emoji? expand_more

An optional extra lock on top of the secret link. The organiser picks one of 35 emoji, and anyone opening the calendar or Hub must pick the same one first. The emoji is written next to the link when the organiser copies, shares, emails or prints it. Your browser remembers it for 30 days.

A calendar in a Hub uses the Hub's emoji unless it has its own. Three wrong picks lock that device out for 5 minutes, then longer.

Can the organiser block someone? expand_more

Yes. In the People list, the more menu beside a name opens Manage person: choose Block and confirm, and that person sees "The organiser has blocked you from this calendar" while their answers are left out of the calendar and Best Match (their bookings still hold their places). Choose Unblock the same way to restore them. Nothing is deleted. The same menu can remove a person or report them to the super admin.

I don't know or forgot the emoji. What now? expand_more

Ask the organiser - it is usually in the message or poster with the link. Organisers can see and change it in Settings on any device where they are signed in. If the organiser forgot it too, the site admin can remove it.

How long do calendars last? expand_more

Calendars with no activity (saves) are automatically deleted after 40 days to reduce data storage. Make sure someone saves their availability periodically to keep the calendar active, or export your results before the calendar expires.

Can I edit the calendar settings after creation? expand_more

Currently, calendar settings (title, dates, slots, status options) cannot be changed after creation. Create a new calendar if you need different settings.

Is my data secure? expand_more

The calendar link is the invite - anyone signed in who has the link can view and join. Only share links with trusted participants. Your name belongs to your account, so nobody else can change your availability. Do not enter sensitive or private information.

Why don't I see the organiser indicators? expand_more

Organiser indicators (coloured left borders on slots) only appear when:

  • You have joined the calendar
  • The organiser has set their availability for that slot
  • You are NOT the organiser (you don't need to see your own preferences)
  • The calendar is Open (not Hidden or Blind)
  • The organiser has not turned them off in Calendar settings

Until you join, you won't see any individual user indicators for privacy reasons.

Does my availability save automatically? expand_more

Yes! Changes auto-save after about 4-5 seconds of inactivity. You'll see the save button spin briefly when saving. You can also click the check button to save immediately.

What do the different status colours mean? expand_more

The legend at the top of the calendar shows what each colour means. By default:

  • Green = Yes / Available
  • Yellow/Orange = Maybe / Tentative
  • Red/Blue = No / Unavailable
  • Grey = No response yet

Organisers can customise these colours when creating the calendar.

Why do I need to join to see who responded? expand_more

For privacy, individual responses are only shown to people who have joined the calendar. This prevents casual visitors from seeing who is available when. On the ranking cards:

  • Not joined: You only see the counts, with no names
  • Joined (Open mode): You'll see the full roster with names and statuses
  • Joined (Hidden mode): Only the organiser sees the roster
  • Joined (Blind mode): No one sees individual responses, and names are private

Aggregate counts (e.g., "3 Yes, 2 Maybe") are always visible to everyone.

What is a Calendar Hub? expand_more

A Hub is one private link that lists multiple calendars. Share the Hub once; participants pick which calendar to open. You join each calendar separately, using the same account. See the Calendar Hubs section above.

How do I open a calendar or Hub if I only have a code? expand_more

Use the Go page. Choose Calendar or Hub, paste the code or full link, and tap Go.

How do I sign out? expand_more

Tap your name in the top corner to open your profile page, then tap Sign out. Sign-ins last 30 days on each device unless you sign out. Signing out does not remove your names from any calendar.

How do I delete a participant? (Organiser) expand_more

Organisers can remove any participant (except themselves) from the calendar:

  1. Click the person person icon to open the user list
  2. Find the participant you want to remove
  3. Click the delete trash icon next to their name
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted

Warning: This permanently deletes the participant and all their availability data. This action cannot be undone.

Why can't I change my availability? expand_more

If you see a green "Decided" banner at the top, the organiser has locked in a final date and slot. The calendar is frozen and no one can change their availability. You can still view all the responses and rankings, but the slots are read-only. If the organiser reopens the calendar, you will be able to edit again.

How do I choose a final date as the organiser? expand_more

Once enough people have responded:

  1. Open the calendar signed in to the organiser account
  2. Expand a rank card by tapping "View"
  3. Tap the green Choose button
  4. Confirm your choice

This freezes the calendar. To undo, tap the Reopen button in the green decided banner.

How do I change the ranking sort order? expand_more

Anyone who has joined can use the toggle buttons at the top of the rankings section:

  • By Match - Shows dates with the highest combined scores first (default)
  • By Date - Shows dates in chronological order (earliest first)

You can also change how many results are shown using the 2 / 10 / All buttons. When the organiser changes the sort order, it is saved as the default for all users. Other users can change it locally during their session.

build Maintenance

Inactive calendars are removed automatically by cleanup.php. Schedule it via cron on your server (daily at low-traffic hours is recommended): 

0 3 * * * /usr/bin/php /path/to/cleanup.php >> /var/log/grape_cleanup.log 2>&1

Replace /path/to/cleanup.php with the absolute path on your host. The script should run from CLI only (not via the web). It prunes expired sessions, old rate-limit rows, and calendars past the auto-delete threshold set in Admin → Setup.
