Creative ways to use When RU Free? beyond simple scheduling. Use these templates to get started quickly!

Get creative! The flexible Slots + Options system can adapt to almost any group availability event, and more! Think of Slots as "what/when" and Options as "my response" -- then combine them to fit your needs. Use the Date display filter ("Only chosen" or "All except") to narrow which dates participants see within your date range.

sports_soccer Weekend Sports Tryouts

Non-default settings used: Custom title, Sport category, 1-month range, exclude weekends, "Only chosen" date filter, custom slots, 4 custom options, and Weights scoring (3, 2, 1, 0). After creating, open the calendar and click date headers to select which tryout dates participants will see.

how_to_vote Voting & Polls

Use When RU Free? as a simple voting or polling tool. There are two main approaches depending on your needs:

Method 1 Choices as Options

Use a single Slot (called "Vote" or "Poll") and put your voting choices as the Options. People cycle through to pick their choice. Best for single-choice votes where people pick ONE option.

Setting Configuration
Date Range 1 day (use the "1 day" quick preset)
Slots One Slot: Vote or Poll
Options Your voting choices (1-5 Options)
Scoring Weights -- set all options to equal weight (e.g., 1 each) so every choice scores the same

Method 2 Choices as time Slots

Put your voting choices/candidates as multiple slots and use simple status options like "Yes" or ranked preferences like "1st, 2nd, 3rd". Best for approval voting, ranking, or when people can select multiple options.

Setting Configuration
Date Range 1 day (use the "1 day" quick preset)
Time Slots Your choices/candidates (1-6 options)
Status Options Simple: Yes only
Or ranked: 1st 2nd 3rd
Scoring For "Yes" only: Weights (1)
For ranked: Weights (3, 2, 1) -- highest points for 1st choice

calendar_add_on Bookings & Reservations

Book times, places, seats and shifts - first come, first served. Share facilities, equipment and spaces, or let people sign up for appointments, sessions and rosters.

Booking forms: templates with People per slot are first come, first served. Each slot can only be taken by that many people, Bookings per person caps how many slots one person can take (per day or in total), and people tap a slot to book it, just like marking availability. You can remove bookings and Close bookings when you're done.
Which limit? 1 in total gives everyone one turn in the whole calendar (interviews, tours, workshops). Per day lets people come back but stops anyone hogging a day (courts, desks, gym). No limit suits shared things people book for as long as they need (holiday house, car, room). Slots don't have to be times - they can be desks, seats or items.
