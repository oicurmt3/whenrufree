Privacy-first by design

If the app does not need it, the app does not store it.

Kept

  • A private username you choose
  • A profile name other people see on calendars
  • Provider IDs as HMAC-SHA-256 keyed hashes
  • Calendar answers, bookings and the calendar link token

Never collected

  • Email address
  • Real name or photo from Google or Facebook
  • Advertising or analytics identifiers
  • Third-party tracking of any kind

Under your control

  • HttpOnly session cookie, 30 days, renewed on use
  • Delete your account: preview first, then confirm with your username
  • Leave a calendar and take your name and answers with you
  • Inactive calendars clean up automatically
