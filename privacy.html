This Privacy Policy explains how When RU Free? (the "Service") handles personal information. This Policy is intended to align with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Important: Calendar and Hub links are invites. Anyone who signs in with the link can view and join (calendars) or see the calendar list (Hubs). Share links only with intended participants.

1. Scope and intended users

The Service is intended for use by people in Australia. If you access the Service from outside Australia, your information will still be handled in accordance with this Policy and Australian privacy law principles, to the extent applicable.

Important: This website far from being 100% secure, so NEVER include any information of a private or sensitive nature. I mean it, you have been warned!

2. What personal information we collect (APP 3 & APP 5)

You need a free account to open, join or create calendars and Hubs. For your account we store:

  • A private username you choose (only you and the site admin see it) and a profile name (shown on calendars you join unless you change it for one calendar).
  • How you sign in:
    • Google: we ask Google only for a sign-in ID (the openid scope). We do not receive or store your email, name or photo.
    • Facebook: we read only your app-scoped Facebook ID. We do not receive or store your email, name, photo or friends.
    • We never store the Google or Facebook ID itself — only a scrambled (keyed hash) copy that cannot be turned back into the ID.
    • Passkeys (fingerprint / face / screen lock): we store a public key for your device, a short device name (for example "iPhone"), and when it was added and last used. Your fingerprint, face or screen lock is checked by your own device and is never sent to us.
  • When the account was created and last used, and a hashed copy of your sign-in session code.

On calendars and Hubs we also store what you and other participants enter:

  • Names on calendars and availability selections (status per date and time slot).
  • Organiser details (the organiser name shown on a calendar or Hub).
  • Hub details (Hub name and organiser name).

The Service also collects calendar configuration information (title, category, time slots, date range, status options, privacy mode, date display filter settings, and any finalised date/slot selection). Hub configuration includes the Hub title. This information may not be personal information on its own, but may become personal information when combined with names and responses.

Old PINs: names and Hubs created before accounts had a 4-digit PIN, stored only as a hashed value. When you claim an old name or Hub with its PIN, the PIN is deleted. Unclaimed PINs are deleted after a transition period.

Access emoji (optional): if the organiser sets one, we store which of the 35 emoji was chosen for that calendar or Hub. When you pick the right emoji, we remember that for your account (and your browser receives a random access code) for 30 days; we store only a hashed copy of the code. To stop guessing, we record wrong picks against the IP address and the calendar or Hub, and delete these records after 30 days without a wrong pick.

Blocking: an organiser can block someone from a calendar. We store that as a flag on the person's name on that calendar; it is removed if the organiser unblocks them or the name is deleted.

Reports: any participant can report someone on a calendar. A report stores the name the reported person was using, the reason and any short note, your name on that calendar (so the organiser knows who reported), the calendar title, and which account was involved. A report about another participant goes to that calendar's organiser; a report about the organiser goes to the site admin. The reported person is not told who reported them. Reports never contain a username on calendars, and the organiser's report inbox never shows one.

3. How we collect personal information

  • From you when you create an account, choose a username and profile name, and add sign-in methods.
  • From Google or Facebook, limited to the sign-in ID described above, when you choose to sign in with them.
  • From you when you create a calendar or Hub, join a calendar, or record availability.
  • Limited technical information may be processed by the server to operate and secure the Service (for example, request logs). We do not use this information for advertising.

4. Why we collect, hold, use and disclose personal information (APP 6)

We collect and use personal information for the primary purpose of operating the Service, including to:

  • Sign you in and show your calendars and Hubs on your profile page.
  • Create and display calendars and Hubs and allow participants to record availability.
  • Provide ranking and aggregation features based on participant selections.
  • Apply access controls (accounts, the optional access emoji, and blocking) and reduce misuse.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, and secure the Service.

We do not use personal information for direct marketing, and there is no tracking or advertising.

5. Disclosure of personal information (APP 6)

The Service is designed so calendars and Hubs are not published publicly. You need to be signed in and have the unique link (plus the emoji, if the organiser set one) to open a calendar or Hub. Anyone signed in with a calendar link can view and join that calendar. Anyone signed in with a Hub link can view the list of calendars in that Hub; only the Hub organiser can add calendars unless the organiser turns on Let other people add their own calendars. Other participants see the name you use on that calendar — never your username or how you sign in. On Blind calendars, and on Hidden calendars unless the organiser switches on Show everyone who has joined, only the organiser sees who has joined.

We may disclose personal information:

  • To other participants you share the calendar or Hub link with (because they can access the Service via that link).
  • To service providers who support hosting, infrastructure, or security of the Service (see "Overseas disclosure").
  • If required or authorised by law (for example, in response to a valid legal request).

When you sign in with Google or Facebook, you use their sign-in page and their own privacy policies apply to that step. We do not send them your calendar data.

6. Overseas disclosure (APP 8)

The Service may use hosting, storage, or security providers that operate or store data outside Australia. This means personal information may be disclosed to, or accessible from, overseas locations.

Where practicable, we take reasonable steps to ensure overseas recipients handle personal information in a manner consistent with this Policy.

7. Security (APP 11)

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference, loss, unauthorised access, modification, or disclosure. Security measures may include request validation, access controls, and secure configuration.

HTTPS and link sharing

This service is intended to be accessed over HTTPS. HTTPS encrypts data in transit between your browser and the server and helps protect calendar information from interception or modification.

Calendar links are invites. Anyone signed in with the link can view and join the calendar. Hub links reveal the list of calendars in that Hub. For this reason, links should only be shared with intended participants and should not be posted publicly or forwarded unnecessarily.

Sign-in sessions are kept in a secure, HttpOnly cookie for 30 days (renewed while you use the Service); we store only a hashed copy of the session code.

8. Data retention and deletion

Calendars may be automatically deleted after extended inactivity (for example, where there has been no Submit/Save activity for a period of time). This is intended to reduce data retention.

Accounts that have not been used for about two years, and that have no names on calendars and no Hubs, are deleted automatically.

Reports between participants on a calendar are deleted with that calendar. Reports about an organiser are kept after the calendar is gone so they can still be reviewed, and decided reports are kept for up to 180 days as a record before they are deleted.

If you need a permanent record of arrangements, you should export or record outcomes outside the Service.

8a. Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account yourself at any time:

  1. Sign in and open your profile page.
  2. Under Delete account, check the list of what will be removed, type your username and press Delete my account.

This immediately deletes your account, its sign-in links (Google, Facebook, passkeys) and sessions, your names and availability on every calendar, every calendar and Hub you organise (for all participants), and your remembered access emoji picks.

You can also remove a single sign-in method (for example Facebook) on the profile page, as long as one method remains. If you signed in with Facebook and removed our app in your Facebook settings, you can still delete your data here, or email admin@whenrufree.net and we will delete it within 30 days.

Important: We can, and likely will, delete your calendar at any time, for no reason. This means don't rely on this website to keep your availability calendar or keep this service active.

9. Access and correction (APP 12 & APP 13)

You may request access to personal information we hold about you and request correction if it is inaccurate, out-of-date, incomplete, irrelevant, or misleading. Requests should be made using the contact details below.

10. Cookies and local storage

The Service uses only essential cookies and browser storage. There are no tracking or advertising cookies.

  • Sign-in cookie (wruf_account): keeps you signed in for up to 30 days, renewed while you use the Service.
  • Sign-in step cookie (wruf_oauth): a short-lived (10 minute) cookie used only while you sign in with Google or Facebook, to protect that step.
  • Security session cookie: used for security controls such as CSRF protection and passkey sign-in.
  • Browser storage: unsaved availability changes (kept until they are sent to the server) and your access emoji picks (a random code, for up to 30 days).

On shared computers, use Sign out on your profile page when you finish.

11. Complaints and enquiries (APP 1)

If you have a privacy enquiry, want to request access/correction, or want to make a privacy complaint, contact: admin@whenrufree.net

We aim to acknowledge and respond to privacy enquiries and complaints within 30 days.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC). OAIC website (plain text): www.oaic.gov.au

We may update this Policy from time to time. The updated version will be published on this page.
